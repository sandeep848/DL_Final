\section{Introduction and Constraints}
This report presents our ultra-fine methodology and empirical evaluation for the Image Geolocation Challenge. The task is to predict exact decimal GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) of street-level photos taken across 12 European countries (Belarus, Finland, France, Germany, Iceland, Italy, Norway, Poland, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, United Kingdom).

Our submission complies strictly with all competition constraints:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Parameter Constraint:} The submitted \textbf{RegNet-Y 400MF} architecture contains \textbf{4,500,282 trainable parameters}, fully respecting the hard limit of $\le 5,000,000$ parameters (safety margin of 499,718 parameters).
    \item \textbf{Data Policy:} The model is trained \textbf{strictly from scratch} (random initialization) using exclusively the provided dataset of $\sim$11,758 training images. No pre-trained ImageNet weights, external datasets, or synthetic images were used.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methodology}

\subsection{512-Cell Voronoi Spatial Discretization \& 3D Spherical Coordinate Mapping}
To avoid latitudinal metric distortion across Europe, coordinates $(\text{lat}, \text{lng})$ are mapped onto 3D unit sphere Cartesian vectors $(x, y, z)$. Europe is discretized into $K=512$ fine-grained spatial Voronoi cells via 3D K-Means clustering. At $K=512$, each spatial cell contains $\sim$23 training photos, providing an ideal trade-off between sample density and local resolution (~25 km average cell radius, oracle median error 25.97 km).

\subsection{Spatially-Constrained Local Neighborhood Softmax Decoding}
Standard global soft expectation across distant regions causes \textit{multimodal spatial averaging failure} (e.g., averaging probabilities between France and Finland to produce a fictitious point in Germany). 

To eliminate this failure mode, we propose \textbf{Spatially-Constrained Local Neighborhood Softmax Decoding}. Let $c^* = \arg\max_k z_k$ denote the top-1 predicted cell. Cell logits outside a local radius $R_{\text{local}} = 150\text{ km}$ of $c^*$ are masked out ($z_k \to -\infty$), and temperature-scaled softmax ($\tau = 0.25$) is evaluated exclusively over the local neighborhood:
\begin{equation}
P(c_k \mid x) = \frac{\exp(z_k / \tau) \cdot \mathbb{I}[d(c_k, c^*) \le R_{\text{local}}]}{\sum_{j} \exp(z_j / \tau) \cdot \mathbb{I}[d(c_j, c^*) \le R_{\text{local}}]}
\end{equation}
Coordinates are then refined via continuous Tanh local offset regression bounded within $\pm 2.0^\circ$ latitude ($\sim$200 km local neighborhood):
\begin{equation}
\hat{\text{lat}} = \text{soft\_lat} + \Delta z_1 \cdot 2.0^\circ, \quad \hat{\text{lng}} = \text{soft\_lng} + \Delta z_2 \cdot \frac{2.0^\circ}{\max(\cos(\text{soft\_lat}_{\text{rad}}), 0.2)}
\end{equation}

\subsection{RegNet-Y 400MF Backbone, GeM Pooling \& Multi-Task Supervision}
The backbone architecture uses \textbf{RegNet-Y 400MF} ($3.90\text{M}$ backbone parameters), which utilizes standard Conv+BatchNorm+ReLU residual blocks with Squeeze-and-Excitation modules to ensure fast, stable convergence when training from scratch without pre-trained weights. Standard global average pooling is upgraded with \textbf{Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling} ($p=3.0$) to emphasize salient localized visual features (architectural motifs, road signs, landscape elements).

The multi-task loss objective combines cell classification (Cross-Entropy with label smoothing 0.05), country classification (weight 0.3), direct 3D Cartesian unit-sphere MSE (weight 0.5), and \textbf{Focal Log-Haversine Geodesic Loss} (weight 1.5):
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{focal\_log\_hav}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \left(1 - \exp\left(-\frac{d_{\text{hav}}(\hat{p}_i, p_i)}{150}\right)\right) \cdot \log\left(1 + \frac{d_{\text{hav}}(\hat{p}_i, p_i)}{10.0}\right)
\end{equation}
Optimization uses AdamW ($\text{lr}=10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$) over 120 epochs with a 5-epoch linear warmup and Cosine Annealing decay down to $10^{-5}$.

\section{10-View Multi-Crop Test-Time Augmentation (TTA)}
Inference on test images in \texttt{holdout\_public/} uses \textbf{10-View Multi-Crop TTA} (5 spatial crops + 5 horizontal flips at $384 \times 384$) to average logit predictions before applying local neighborhood soft expectation, eliminating spatial jitter and yielding accurate, robust predictions.
